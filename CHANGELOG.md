# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Unreleased

First public build, for Windows and Android.

- Capture a region from anywhere with one global shortcut. Press Space to switch to window capture.
- Auto-balance trims uniform borders and adds even padding. The background is sampled from the screenshot's edges.
- Auto-redact finds email addresses, card numbers (Luhn-checked), IPv4 and IPv6 addresses, and API keys, then covers them with blur or a solid bar.
- Copy text from any screenshot with on-device OCR. Nothing is uploaded.
- Export PNG, JPEG or WebP at 1x, 2x or 3x, with presets for X, LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube.
- Quick send between a PC and a phone on the same network, encrypted, with no account.
- Free with every feature, 3 saved presets and batch for up to 5 images. Exports carry a small watermark.
- Pro ($11, one time) removes the watermark, adds your own brand mark, lifts the preset and batch limits, and includes a year of updates.
